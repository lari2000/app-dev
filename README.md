# League of Legends

## About
**League of Legends (LoL)** is a *multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA)* developed and published by *Riot Games*. Players compete in teams of five to destroy the opposing team’s Nexus while defending their own.

---

## Gameplay
> Choose your champion, master your lane, and work with your team to achieve victory.

### Game Modes
1. Summoner’s Rift  
2. ARAM (All Random All Mid)  
3. Teamfight Tactics  

### Roles
- Top Lane  
- Jungle  
- Mid Lane  
- ADC  
- Support  

---

## Features
`Free-to-play`
- Over 160 champions to choose from
- Ranked ladder system
- Regular updates and events
- Esports tournaments and world championships
